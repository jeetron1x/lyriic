## 🌟 What is Lyriic?

**Lyriic** is a desktop music experience designed for mindful listening on Windows. Combining synchronized typography with atmospheric ambient backlighting and smoky glass aesthetics, Lyriic transforms your desktop into a calm, focused stage for your favorite music.

For full demonstrations, interactive previews, and installer downloads, please visit the official website:
👉 **[jeetron1x.github.io/lyriic](https://jeetron1x.github.io/lyriic/)**

---

## ✨ Experience Highlights

- **Fluid Synchronized Lyrics**: Real-time word-by-word highlighted lyrics that follow the rhythm of the track seamlessly.
- **Atmospheric Ambient Lighting**: Audio-reactive edge lighting and subtle smoke backdrops that illuminate in harmony with your music.
- **Desktop Overlay**: A minimal translucent floating mode that stays visible while you work or browse.
- **Custom Playlists & Queue**: Organize your music into personal collections with intuitive queue management.
- **100% Privacy-First**: Zero tracking, zero telemetry. Your library, listening habits, and playlists stay entirely on your local machine.
